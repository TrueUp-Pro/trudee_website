Straight Answers

What Shop Owners Ask Us

Do my techs have to install anything?

No. They text a video to a number, the same way they text you now. Nothing to install, no account to create, no password to reset at 7am. That's the whole point—rollout is telling your crew a phone number.

Does this replace ServiceTitan or Housecall Pro?

No, and we're not trying to. Those run your dispatch, your invoicing, and your customer records. Trudee produces the field documentation that gets attached to the job. If you already run field software, the PDF goes into it.

What if the video is dark, loud, or shaky?

Crawlspaces are dark and mechanical rooms are loud—that's the normal case, not the exception. Trudee works from what your tech says as much as what the camera sees, so narrating matters more than camera work. Where something isn't clear, the report says so rather than guessing.

Is a report good enough to send a customer as-is?

That's what it's built for—an objective, evidence-based document with your branding on it. Read it before it goes out, the same as you'd read anything with your company name on it.

What does it cost?

Everyone gets 5 free reports. Fill out the beta signup and we'll add 20 more, for 25 free. Paid plans start at $19/mo at launch—see pricing.

Who can see our videos and reports?

Your reports are yours. We use them to run your account, not to advertise to your customers or resell to your competitors. The details are in our privacy policy.
