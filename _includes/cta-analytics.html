{% comment %} Click tracking for the tap-to-text CTA, loaded once per page from the layout. This matters for ad measurement: making the number the primary action means a visitor who converts by texting never submits the form, so the pixel's CompleteRegistration never fires. Without this, /plumbing/ ad traffic would look like it stopped converting. Meta's `Contact` is the standard event for an off-site contact action, so campaigns still have a signal to optimize on. Note this measures INTENT, not a completed opt-in — we cannot see whether they actually sent the message. Treat it as top-of-funnel, and reconcile against real opt-ins on the SMS side before judging campaign performance. {% endcomment %}
