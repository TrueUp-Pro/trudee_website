Trudee SMS Service

Text OPTIN to +1 (206) 761-0690 to begin using Trudee.

After receiving your confirmation message, send a video from the job and receive a professional field report.

Text OPTIN to +1 (206) 761-0690

How It Works

  1. Text OPTIN to +1 (206) 761-0690.
  2. Receive a confirmation message from Trudee.
  3. Record a video on the job.
  4. Send the video to +1 (206) 761-0690.
  5. Trudee processes the video.
  6. Trudee sends transactional SMS updates about your request.
  7. When your report is ready, Trudee sends a secure link to your field report.

SMS Consent

By texting OPTIN to +1 (206) 761-0690, you consent to receive transactional SMS/MMS messages from Trudee related to report requests and report delivery.

After opting in, users may submit videos for report generation.

Messages may include confirmation that your video was received, processing updates, error notices, and a secure link to your completed report.

Message frequency varies based on your activity. Message and data rates may apply. Reply HELP for help. Reply STOP to opt out.

Example Opt-In

To begin using Trudee:

Text OPTIN to +1 (206) 761-0690.

You will receive a confirmation message and may then submit videos for report generation.

Message Frequency

Message frequency varies based on user activity. Most video submissions result in 1–5 messages, including receipt confirmation, processing updates, and report delivery.

Help and Opt-Out

For help, reply HELP.

To stop receiving messages, reply STOP. You may also use other standard opt-out keywords such as CANCEL, END, QUIT, or UNSUBSCRIBE.

Non-Marketing Use

Trudee messages are transactional and are sent only in response to a user-initiated video submission. Trudee does not use this SMS service to send marketing or promotional messages.
